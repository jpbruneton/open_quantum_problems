# Proposal identifiers — 9 November 2026

The AI-suggested problems on the [New problems](https://openquantumproblems.com/#new-problems) page were numbered P1–P81 and S1–S24 in order of drafting. Promotions to the catalogue had left gaps, and the numbers said nothing about the area. From 9 November 2026 they follow the catalogue's own scheme.

## Rule

- **Catalogue entries** keep their identifiers: area code + number (M2, QF10, G6, H1, QG6, R4…).
- **Proposals** take a tier letter, then the area code, then a number in order within that area: **P** for a priority candidate (PE2 is the second entanglement candidate), **S** for a secondary lead. A secondary lead belongs to the area of the first entry it extends.
- Identifiers are never reused. A proposal promoted to the catalogue takes the next free entry number in its area and leaves the proposals page; its former identifier is recorded in the dated report of the promotion.
- The former keys are kept in the data (`formerly`) and still open the same page: `#np/P20` and `#np/PU2` show the same proposal.

Dated reports written before this change (for example [2026-11-08-new-problems-proposal.md](2026-11-08-new-problems-proposal.md)) keep the former keys. Use the tables below to translate them.
