# New Problems review · 27 November 2026

All 79 proposals in the [19 November selection](2026-11-19-proposal-selection.md) were compared individually with the catalogue and its [claimed resolutions](https://openquantumproblems.com/claimed-solved), [partial results](https://openquantumproblems.com/improved), and [New Research notes](https://openquantumproblems.com/new-research). The comparison includes newly formulated catalogue questions as well as changes of status. The decisions below concern the material on these pages through 27 November. They do not constitute a new literature survey or an independent verification of the underlying proofs.

The original 49 priority proposals and 21 secondary leads are retained, with PN4 refocused on dimension five. Nine withdrawals still stand. PE5 and PN6 bring the active list to **51 priority proposals and 21 secondary leads**. The main catalogue counts are unaffected.

The dated catalogue reviews give the evidence for the changes in [measurements, gravity and many-body systems](2026-11-20-literature-update.md), [ququart entropy, thermal attenuators, Gibbs samplers and classical oracles](2026-11-23-literature-update.md), [NPT distillability, finite-copy estimation and two-way capacities](2026-11-26-literature-update.md), and [spin Wigner negativity and Rabi spectral bounds](2026-11-27-literature-update.md). F18 already records the residual minimum negative volume, and M18 already asks for the remaining spectral inequality at higher levels. Repeating those residuals would add no distinct test.

## Individual decisions

The [tetrahedral discrimination note](../../research/entries/local-discrimination-entangled-qubits/README.md) gives a strict one-message gap and explicitly leaves two-way protocols open. PE5 fixes a single angle and allows limits of protocols. PN6 is an inferred successor to the [N19 two-copy example](2026-11-26-literature-update.md), with lower confidence about its bibliographic status. Targeted searches for the tetrahedral ensemble and three-copy SLD saturation, including the trureturing issue and pull-request index, yielded no matching resolution. PE6 was added after a separate review in which the six sources it cites (arXiv:0910.4151v5, 1010.1750v5, 1210.3181v2, 1410.4184v4, 1703.03392v2, 2304.14878v2) were read in full and both ends of the interval were rederived; arXiv searches to 27 November 2026, trureturing and Formal Conjectures found no narrowing of $[1,2]$ and no source stating the optimal exponent as open. PC4 was added after a separate review in which its six sources (arXiv:1108.5357v3, 1807.11939v3, 2201.09257v2, 2610.00187v1, 2609.28592v1 and the OpenAI generalized amplitude-damping manuscript) were read in full; the Choi-state formulas for generalized amplitude damping were checked numerically, and arXiv and trureturing searches found no resolution.

For PN4, the [ququart manuscript and dimension-five calculations](../../research/entries/complete-mub-entropy-ququart/README.md) give the lower bound and the candidate distribution.
