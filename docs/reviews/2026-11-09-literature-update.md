# Literature update — 9 November 2026: four new areas and 31 new entries

This update adds 31 entries to the catalogue. They come from the review of new
problems of the same day ([2026-11-09-new-problems-new-areas.md](2026-11-09-new-problems-new-areas.md)),
first published as proposals and then promoted at the maintainer's request: most of
them are well-known, long-standing open problems, which belong in the catalogue rather
than on the "New problems suggested by AI" page.

## New areas

| Code | Area | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| G | Black holes & semiclassical gravity | G1–G10 |
| H | Holography & gravitational complexity | H1–H6 |
| QG | Quantum gravity & cosmology | QG1–QG8 |
| R | Renormalization group & critical phenomena | R1–R4 |

Three entries join Foundations: F13 (relativistic collapse), F14 (causal nonlinear
quantum mechanics), F15 (higher-order interference).

Status choices. G3 and H3 are "Improved": Hintz–Klein (state independence of the
leading divergence, under mode stability) and Lee–Pilatowsky-Cameo–Choi (sustained,
not linear, complexity growth) are partial results on the stated problems. H1 stays
"Open": Tierz's unassessed no-go claim (arXiv:2512.00361v1) concerns only the
non-chiral part (b). Easson's (G3), Xu's (G10) and Aziz–Howl's (QG6) claims are
reported as context, not as resolutions.

## Remaining proposals

Six candidates of the same review stay on the new-problems page, filed under the new
areas: P58 (quantum Bousso bound beyond weak gravity) and P59 (Rényi QNEC) extend G6;
P55 (Virasoro ETH), P56 (boundary rigidity from RT entropies) and P64 (six-region
holographic entropy cone) under Holography; P81 (non-classical gravitational radiation)
extends QG6.

## Verification

Each entry carries one dated, versioned evidence item whose arXiv page (title,
authors, submission history, DOI where shown) was opened on 9 November 2026.
That check corrected three attributions carried by the source review:

- arXiv:2308.14517 is Kay's review "Quantum field theory in curved spacetime (2nd
  edition)", not a review by Hollands and Wald. The statement attributed to it in the
  source review is no longer cited.
- arXiv:2112.04248 is Aizenman's "A geometric perspective on the scaling limits of
  critical Ising and φ⁴_d models", not a paper by Gunaratnam, Panagiotis, Panis and
  Severo.
- arXiv:2310.19655 (Hintz–Klein) has a DOI in Class. Quantum Grav., not PRD 109,
  L061702 as the source review stated.

Other references come from the source review, which checked identifiers but not
every title or journal; labels are kept to author, topic and year where the title
was not rechecked. No proof was audited.
