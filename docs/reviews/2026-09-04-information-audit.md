# Catalogue audit: entanglement, nonlocality and channels

Audit date: 4 September 2026. Scope: all 35 entries in `app/data/cat-entanglement.js`, `app/data/cat-nonlocality.js` and `app/data/cat-channels.js`. This is an investigation and editorial recommendation, not an implementation or an independent verification of new mathematical proofs.

## Executive findings

The subjects remain important, but the catalogue mixes sharp conjectures, broad research programmes, equivalent reductions and already-impossible universal requests. Recommended disposition: **9 keep, 23 reframe, 3 merge, 0 retire outright**. “Keep” does not endorse every sentence of the present entry. “Merge” concerns catalogue structure, not a claim that the underlying science is solved. Cross-category consolidation retains C10 as the landing page and merges M11 into it.

1. **N3 contains two definite mathematical errors.** The relationship between complete mutually unbiased bases (MUBs) and finite projective planes is conjectural, not a proved equivalence. Nonexistence of a projective plane of order 10 was proved in 1989; it is not an open example. These are corrections, not matters of editorial taste. Sources: [original proposed connection](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0403057), [Lam–Thiel–Swiercz theorem](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1989-049-4).
2. **N7 understates an established 2026 result and includes an ill-posed mixed-state target.** Every pure multipartite entangled qubit state can now be self-tested in the standard Bell setting. Arbitrary local dimension remains a meaningful frontier. Conventional exact self-testing cannot identify a genuinely mixed bipartite target uniquely in the same way; local isometries, irrelevant auxiliary systems and complex-conjugation ambiguities must be specified. Sources: [March 2026 theorem](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-70829-x), [bipartite pure-state theorem and mixed-state obstruction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5458560/).
3. **N9 asks generically for an algorithm ruled out by established results.** The unrestricted Bell-game problem needs restriction, not another universal solver. Conversely, **C1 must not infer ordinary memoryless quantum/private capacity uncomputability from zero-error or other channel models**. Nonadditivity, computational hardness and uncomputability are different statements. Sources: [MIP*=RE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04383), [2026 capacity-complexity paper explicitly separating these issues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22471).
4. **E12 misses a complete pure-state marginal hierarchy; E3 overextends Gaussian PPT sufficiency.** A complete hierarchy of semidefinite tests for pure-state marginal compatibility is already known, though not an efficient universal algorithm. General mixed-state compatibility with an explicitly represented global matrix is itself semidefinite feasibility. Gaussian PPT is not a sufficient separability test in arbitrary bipartite mode configurations. Sources: [complete hierarchy](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-20799-5), [Gaussian separability scope](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.05215).
5. **Channel statements need operational hypotheses.** The quantum-limited amplifier already has an exact unrestricted-energy two-way capacity; it should not be included indiscriminately among unresolved noisy amplifiers. A strong converse can fail under a mean-energy constraint even for the pure-loss classical channel; changing the energy constraint changes the question. Sources: [PLOB theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.08863), [mean-energy counterexample and occupation-constraint distinction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.6732).
6. **E2 has verifiable citation/provenance errors, not merely debatable progress classification.** arXiv:2607.24479 is by Bharti, Gajjala and Haug, not Chen, Đoković and Johnston. arXiv:2607.24309 is by Fraser, Huber, Pozsgay and Vona, not Cariello and Ferraz. Fu–Gao–Park v2 explicitly added an AI statement and discusses proof-development use of GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6 Sol; the current no-disclosure description is incorrect for that version. The mathematical conclusions remain preprint claims until separately assessed. Sources: [Bharti et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24479), [Fraser et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24309), [Fu et al. v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21367v2).
7. **The largest completeness gaps are concrete, not more umbrella topics:** PPT-squared, faithfulness of distillable secret key, exact depolarizing capacity, and Bell-locality/activation benchmarks. SICs and MUBs deserve retention but fit measurement geometry better than a category named only “nonlocality”. These are editorial judgments informed by the evidence below.

## Disposition of every current entry

Evidence language: “theorem” refers to an established published result; “preprint” means an author-claimed result in a manuscript inspected here, not a proof certified by this audit; “judgment” is a recommendation about scope or catalogue organization. Mathematical consequences of stated definitions are distinguished from new literature claims.

### Entanglement: E1–E15

| ID | Decision | Reason and required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Keep | NPT bound entanglement remains a precise foundational existence question. Replace the loose claim of verification for “many copies” with quantified, parameter-dependent finite-copy statements; these do not establish all-copy undistillability of a fixed NPT state. [Tabia–Chen–Hsieh, August 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08836) concerns a two-parameter DiVincenzo-family state and neighborhoods, not a solution of NPT distillability or the Werner conjecture. |
| E2 | Merge | Treat Werner distillability as the canonical reduction/benchmark under E1, retaining an addressable child section rather than counting the equivalent conjecture twice. With rho=(I+alpha F)/(d²+alpha d), state the unresolved NPT one-copy-undistillable range explicitly: -1/2 <= alpha < -1/d, d>=3. Three July preprints claim the two-copy theorem; correct the two author lists and Fu v2 AI disclosure noted above. The [Wu–Zou three-copy preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02647) proves special cases only; do not promote it to the full three-copy statement. |
| E3 | Reframe | “Effective separability” must specify norm, promise gap, input representation and dimension scaling. “Maximal tractable classes” is not a defined mathematical output. Gaussian PPT sufficiency requires restrictions: [Gaussian entanglement revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.05215) treats the actual scope, while multipartite-mode Gaussian bound entanglement defeats the blanket wording. [Malavolta's July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23773) is a constant-Euclidean-gap result, not polynomial-time exact or arbitrary-trace-distance separability. |
| E4 | Reframe | Keep an operational membership problem, but distinguish finite-round LOCC, unrestricted LOCC and its closure, maps versus instruments, and epsilon-approximation norm. Their topological differences are established in [Everything You Always Wanted to Know About LOCC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.4583). The [Zhao–Chen August preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18421) concerns multiqubit orthogonal product bases and a finite-round discrimination criterion, not general channel membership. |
| E5 | Reframe | Asymptotic conversion of arbitrary mixed states is a programme, not a single readily falsifiable conjecture. Specify a pair/family, LOCC rate, direction and vanishing-error convention. Ordinary rates, catalytic rates and sublinear auxiliary-resource assistance are distinct tasks; move the latter hypotheses to E11. The known irreversibility motivating E6 already prevents a universal entropy-only reversible conversion law under ordinary LOCC. |
| E6 | Keep | A structural characterization of states satisfying E_C=E_D under a fixed LOCC convention is meaningful. Include pure and reversible locally flagged subclasses; irreversible examples alone do not classify the boundary. [Ao–Philip–Streltsov, August preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20063) concerns PPT operations with correlated catalysis, so it is related evidence, not the missing LOCC characterization. Avoid a generic “second law” claim without fixing the operation class. |
| E7 | Keep | Finite reversible generating sets for multipartite pure-state entanglement remain a sharp question after fixing party number and asymptotic LOCC. Correct attribution: BPRST introduced the programme; the inadequacy of the three EPR pairs plus GHZ candidate follows from [Acín–Vidal–Cirac](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0202056). Restricted one-way/finite-copy impossibility results must not be stated as exclusion of every finite asymptotic LOCC generating set. |
| E8 | Reframe | Multipartite classification is valuable as a programme but is underspecified as a sharp open problem. The entry targets mixed states while invoking mostly pure-state SLOCC classifications. Fix equivalence relation, party/local dimensions, rank and desired output. The familiar nine four-qubit families are parameterized families, not nine equivalence classes. A complete classification across all resource conventions is not a single canonical answer. |
| E9 | Reframe | Separate Turing computability with rational input and certified additive error from efficiency, analytic formulae and additivity. Regularization by itself proves none of these impossible. Entanglement of purification measures total correlations, so it should not be described indiscriminately as an entanglement monotone. [Beigi–Rubboli–Tomamichel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05696), published 2026, gives structural single-copy additivity criteria, not general computability of every regularized quantity. |
| E10 | Keep | Additivity of ordinary von Neumann entanglement of purification remains a concrete question. Include [Chen–Winter's numerical nonadditivity evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.1307) as numerical evidence, not a proved counterexample. The [Negari–Baghali Khanian August preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28897) establishes Rényi results on either side of alpha=1 for specified families, not resolution at alpha=1. Move the broader computation of the whole regularization to E9. |
| E11 | Reframe | Exact product-return catalysts, catalysts returned only in marginal, and approximate return/embezzlement are different resource theories. State error metric, catalyst-size dependence and correlation allowance; unconstrained approximation can trivialize a purported obstruction. [Kondra–Datta–Streltsov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11136) gives the entropy-based pure-bipartite approximate-conversion result under its specific catalyst convention, not a universal mixed/multipartite theorem. The August PPT-catalysis paper in E6 must not be counted twice as independent progress. |
| E12 | Reframe | Split mixed-global compatibility, pure-global compatibility and efficient succinct-input approximation. Mixed-global compatibility is a finite SDP when the full global matrix is represented; exponential size is the obstacle, not lack of a complete formulation. A [complete SDP hierarchy for pure-state marginals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02124) is already available. QMA-completeness requires an appropriate promise formulation; “find a complete criterion” without efficiency restrictions is therefore outdated. |
| E13 | Reframe | State whether the target is the dimension-unrestricted closed quantum entropy cone and whether inequalities are unconstrained and linear. Nontrivial constrained inequalities have long been known; the sharp frontier is not simply finding any “constrained” inequality. [2024 primary treatment](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevA.109.052407) discusses the absence of additional universal unconstrained linear inequalities, while [Tip of the Quantum Entropy Cone](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.240201) concerns finer realizability restrictions. One new inequality would not constitute a complete characterization. |
| E14 | Reframe | Keep AME existence as an incremental parameter-classification programme with explicit unresolved (n,d) cells. Orthogonal-array/classical-MDS equivalences require minimal-support hypotheses; [AME(4,6)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05122) illustrates why general quantum solutions need not obey classical orthogonal-Latin-square obstructions. Planar maximal entanglement is not synonymous with AME. The [AME(7,d) preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01011) and [new large-parameter constructions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05781) update cells, not the complete existence diagram. |
| E15 | Merge | Merge with F7/subsystem structure, or make an explicitly labeled conceptual programme rather than a uniquely answerable conjecture. Identical-particle entanglement depends on operationally accessible algebras, modes and superselection restrictions. The entry's own [Benatti et al. review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06253) already supplies consistency criteria and argues for a mode-based framework; saying that no operationally consistent framework exists overstates the gap. A fixed operational comparison would be a testable replacement. |

### Nonlocality and measurement geometry: N1–N10

| ID | Decision | Reason and required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N1 | Keep | SIC existence in every finite dimension is a genuine flagship conjecture. Distinguish arbitrary existence from Weyl–Heisenberg covariance and additional Zauner symmetry. [Appleby–Flammia–Kopp's constructive Stark approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03970) is conditional, not an unconditional all-dimension theorem. Editorially place SICs with measurement geometry/designs rather than implying that they are Bell-nonlocality problems. |
| N2 | Keep | The maximum number of MUBs in dimension six is an unusually concrete and useful special case; retain a child relationship to N3. The [August order-six Hadamard preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18053) does not by itself certify a fourth MUB: simultaneous mutual unbiasedness of several matrices is additional structure. Neither classification of individual Hadamards nor failure of numerical searches solves M(6). |
| N3 | Reframe | Separate the prime-power complete-set conjecture from computing M(d) for all d. Correct the false plane equivalence and remove order 10 as an open projective-plane case, using the sources in finding 1. The [2026-published composite-dimension review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23997) gives a current baseline. Numerical searches/constructions do not establish universal nonexistence; preserve N2 only as an explicitly nested benchmark rather than unrelated coverage. |
| N4 | Reframe | Distinguish finite-dimensional nonclosure C_q != C_qa from tensor/commuting separation C_qa != C_qc; define how “smallest” orders the setting/output tuple. An [explicit five-input, two-output nonclosure result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05032) should be a baseline. “Highly nonconstructive” is too broad: MIP*=RE supplies effective constructions, even though reducing their size and obtaining transparent examples remain useful objectives. |
| N5 | Reframe | Specify the target tensor-product correlation set, metric, tolerance and chosen families. There cannot be a uniform computable dimension bound sufficient to approximate every unrestricted finite Bell game, by the computability obstruction. In self-testing, a certified minimum target support is not the exact physical dimension because unused auxiliary systems remain invisible. Restricted noise models can admit computable bounds, e.g. [Qin–Yao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10613). |
| N6 | Reframe | Distinguish lower semicomputability, a two-sided additive approximation algorithm, threshold decision and efficient computation. Enumerating finite-dimensional strategies already gives one-sided approximations. The July [Pakhunov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13762), [second Pakhunov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13774) and [Chaturvedi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14569) preprints concern nontermination of fixed NPA levels for whole sets/neighborhoods: they do not prove that each individual Bell functional lacks a finite certificate or that the (2,2,2) value is uncomputable. |
| N7 | Reframe | Update the resolved qubit pure-multipartite case and target arbitrary local dimension in the standard single-source Bell model. State equivalence under local isometries and complex conjugation. Ordinary mixed-state uniqueness is obstructed, not simply an untouched analogous question. Measurement claims also need scope: [all real projective measurements can be self-tested](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00974), but this is not identical to arbitrary complex POVMs under a strict isometry-only definition. Network protocols allowing auxiliary parties/resources answer a different question. |
| N8 | Keep | Optimal robust self-testing bounds are meaningful when a target, Bell normalization and error metric are fixed. Square-root norm errors and linear infidelity errors are not competing exponents in the same convention. Include [Kaniewski's tight three-qubit Mermin result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.08176) among solved benchmarks. The [31 August GHZ preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30851) claims size-independent bounds within a factor of two, not a universal exact optimum. |
| N9 | Merge | Merge unrestricted-algorithm discussion into N6 and dimensional issues into N5; an always-terminating general Bell-maximum solver is ruled out, not an open deliverable. A finite list of selected inequalities could instead become a benchmark section. Its repeated NPA update should not double-count the same papers as progress on two independent questions. Graph-theoretic contextuality optimization with explicitly fixed compatibility constraints is not automatically the same undecidable problem. |
| N10 | Reframe | Contextuality as a computational resource is relevant, but a universal necessary-and-sufficient speedup criterion is too broad without a computational model, free operations, asymptotic input family and classical simulation task. Contextuality necessity in one restricted model does not imply sufficiency in all models. Define a concrete resource-conversion or simulation threshold question and cross-link A16 rather than treating every kind of quantum advantage as one problem. |

### Channels and capacities: C1–C10

| ID | Decision | Reason and required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Reframe | Keep capacity evaluation and structural single-letter/additivity criteria, but separate them from U4 computability. Nonadditivity does not prove that every conceivable single-letter expression is impossible, and ordinary memoryless Q/P uncomputability is not established by the cited specialized undecidability results. [2026 complexity study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22471) explicitly makes this distinction. The [qutrit zero-capacity preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24693) and [depolarizing certificate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15870) are concrete advances, not a general solution. Promote the depolarizing problem below to its own benchmark. |
| C2 | Keep | Energy-constrained quantum capacity of a thermal attenuator is a precise, high-value open problem once transmissivity, environment occupation, input-energy convention and assistance are fixed. Treat private capacity as a separate subtarget. Include known zero-capacity regions and the [2018 bounds](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-06848-0). [Correlated multimode Gaussian coding](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14329-6) already improves upon some single-mode Gaussian strategies; “Gaussian” must not silently mean “product thermal input”. |
| C3 | Reframe | Focus on noisy thermal-loss/amplifier channels and separately specify two-way entanglement capacity Q2 and secret-key capacity K; they need not coincide generically. PLOB already settles the unconstrained-energy quantum-limited amplifier as well as pure loss. Finite-energy capacity is a different problem even for a channel whose unconstrained capacity is known. Use [the primary PLOB theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.08863) to mark solved boundary cases accurately. |
| C4 | Keep | The strong converse for arbitrary memoryless finite-dimensional quantum channels remains sharp with code/assistance and fidelity conventions fixed. The old degradable-channel baseline was a “pretty strong” converse, not the full general claim now made in the [2 August preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01308). Label that manuscript's all-code degradable/antidegradable result as a preprint claim. [Tomamichel's July result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23450) restricts the code family; do not silently replace an all-code theorem with it. |
| C5 | Reframe | Structural separation of private and quantum capacity is meaningful, but “maximum gap” without fixed dimensions/resource normalization is not: tensoring a channel with P>0 and Q=0 makes the absolute gap unbounded. A ratio is also inappropriate when Q=0. Specify dimensions and whether additivity concerns regularized P itself or the single-letter private-information expression. Cross-link bound key rather than collapsing state and channel resource questions. |
| C6 | Reframe | Separate classical graph Shannon capacity, quantum/noncommutative zero-error transmission, entanglement assistance and one-shot versus asymptotic variants. The new [Tandon C7 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30273) is a classical graph-capacity lower bound, not a generic quantum-channel-capacity theorem. State whether reported numbers are Shannon capacity theta(G) or log2(theta(G)) bits/use. The exact capacity of a specified graph is a suitable benchmark inside this broader programme. |
| C7 | Reframe | A broadcast capacity region depends on which receivers need classical/quantum/common/private messages and which entanglement or communication assistance is allowed. Fix one operational task/family before asking for a region. The [August Marton-suboptimality preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19869) is strictly a classical broadcast result: it demonstrates a classical obstruction within the umbrella, not progress on every quantum broadcast coding theorem. |
| C8 | Reframe | Distinguish classical messages over a quantum interference channel from transmission of unknown quantum systems, and fix shared entanglement, cooperation and message pattern. The present references chiefly motivate the former. A general capacity region is a programme; a chosen interference family with an explicit inner/outer-bound gap is a sharper catalogue target. Lack of a general classical solution does not preclude exact quantum special cases. |
| C9 | Reframe | Specify a finite-memory interaction map, representation of parameters, initial memory, access/reset assumptions and stationarity/forgetfulness conditions. Arbitrary non-Markovian channel sequences need not form one finitely specified computable input. Forgetfulness already has operational mathematical definitions; the residual question is capacity calculation or decidability for specified families, with certified finite-block errors and runtime. |
| C10 | Reframe | Absorb M11 into this explicitly labeled infinite-dimensional information programme, preserving channel subtargets under C2/C3. Do not ask for an unconditional strong converse under every “natural” energy condition: [Wilde–Winter](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.6732) gives a mean-energy obstruction, while [photon-occupation constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3287) support a different converse statement. Useful residual questions concern explicit constraints, continuity moduli, compactness and achievable/converse bounds, not recreating all of infinite-dimensional Shannon theory. |

## Prioritized missing or underexposed concrete problems

These are suggested additions after correcting and consolidating current entries, not a recommendation to add every related topic. The first four are publication-ready candidates subject to normal specialist review. The last two need a final targeted status check before a categorical “open” label: the papers establish distinctions and partial progress, but absence of a solution in this search is not a theorem.

### 1. Exact quantum capacity and positivity threshold of the qubit depolarizing channel

Suggested question: For D_q(rho)=(1-q)rho+(q/3)(XrhoX+YrhoY+ZrhoZ), determine the unassisted memoryless quantum capacity Q(D_q), or at least q_* = sup{q in [0,3/4]: Q(D_q)>0}.

Why: This is presently buried in C1 even though it is more concrete than most channel entries. State the noise convention to prevent factors-of-three mistakes. The [August certificate preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15870) gives a 45-copy rank-two positive coherent-information certificate at per-Pauli probability 0.064956, i.e. total error q=0.194868. This is a lower benchmark, not the capacity or critical threshold. Antidegradability gives zero capacity for q>=1/4 within the stated interval; [Tomamichel's 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23450) discusses this regime. Editorial status: promote existing content, not pretend the subject was wholly absent.

### 2. PPT-squared conjecture in complex finite dimension

Suggested question: Is Psi composed with Phi entanglement breaking for every pair of compatible finite-dimensional complex completely positive, trace-preserving PPT maps Phi and Psi?

Why: A canonical sharp channel/entanglement conjecture is missing. It links noisy composition, entanglement swapping and bound entanglement without duplicating E1. The [Christandl–Müller-Hermes–Wolf primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01266) establishes special cases including Gaussian channels; arbitrary higher dimension is the target. Recent sources still address restricted cases: [Prudhoe, revised August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03959), [Park's symplectic-symmetry result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09860), and [An–Lee's July qutrit extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15947).

Important latest-status guardrail: [Park's 13 August preprint, revised 17 August](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13551) claims finite entanglement-breaking index for every PPT channel, plus a uniform index-three result for a restricted family. **Do not add eventual entanglement breaking as an open existence problem.** Finite eventual index does not establish index two. The straightforward real-Hilbert-space analogue is already false; keep “complex” explicit. Current-status confidence: high for a viable conjecture, not a certification that no unindexed September manuscript exists.

### 3. Faithfulness of distillable secret key / entangled states with zero key

Suggested question: Does there exist a finite-dimensional bipartite entangled state rho with K_D(rho)=0 under LOCC with authenticated public classical communication, when the adversary holds a purification?

Why: This is distinct from E1: PPT states can carry distillable secret key, and distillable entanglement implies distillable key. It is also different from maximizing P-Q for channels. Avoid silently equating every definition of positive key cost with entanglement; call this precise version “faithfulness of distillable key” or “mathematical bound key”. The [May 2026 published Cost of quantum secret key](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-05-06-2098) explicitly works under the possible absence of entangled zero-key states and discusses what would happen if such states were found. Its [April 2026 arXiv revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17007) provides the same explicit unresolved conditional framing. A posted [ICFO seminar abstract for 8 September 2026](https://www.icfo.eu/event/4336/stefan-bauml/) still treats these as open questions, but the seminar is future as of this audit and is not evidence of a delivered result. Current-status confidence: high; monitor the forthcoming work before final publication.

### 4. Exact Bell-locality thresholds for two-qubit Werner states

Suggested question: For rho_v=v times the singlet projector +(1-v)I/4, determine the critical v for locality under all single-copy local projective measurements; formulate arbitrary POVMs as a separate target.

Why: The catalogue has abstract correlation-set questions but no comparably accessible canonical noisy-state threshold. The projective problem is related to the order-three Grothendieck constant; do not transfer a projective threshold to POVMs or filtered/multicopy protocols. [Designolle–Vértesi–Pokutta, PRA February 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03739) states that finite-order constants beyond order two are unknown and supplies new certified bounds, carefully distinguished from heuristic candidates. This is a genuinely mathematical benchmark, not simply another numerical search programme. Current-status confidence: high for the unresolved projective constant; check the freshest numerical interval before displaying bounds.

### 5. Universal many-copy activation of Bell nonlocality

Suggested question: For every bipartite entangled rho, is there a finite k for which rho tensor k violates an ordinary Bell inequality using local collective measurements, without an auxiliary entangled state or quantum inputs?

Why: This tests whether entanglement necessarily becomes visible in standard Bell experiments after same-state-copy activation. [Palazuelos](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.3118) proves that superactivation can occur, not that it occurs for every entangled state. [Liang–Masanes–Rosset](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.0548) uses a suitable auxiliary resource for universal activation; [Hirsch et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.02215) shows that single-copy filtering does not reveal all entanglement. The distinction between same-state tensor powers, postselected filters, independent-source networks and auxiliary entanglement is the entire point. Priority: second tier; these sources establish the well-motivated residual formulation, but a specialist/current-status check should precede a definitive 2026 open label.

### 6. Independent-source network nonlocality: exact certification and noise robustness

Suggested target: For a specified binary-output triangle-network correlation family, characterize classical independent-source compatibility and certify an exact quantum realization/nonclassical gap with explicit noise tolerance.

Why: Ordinary Bell scenarios do not cover independence constraints between sources. A new section could host several precise network benchmarks, informed by the [primary network-nonlocality review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.10700). Do not add a generic “does binary-output triangle nonlocality exist?” entry without accounting for [Don et al., May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00981): it claims an analytically nonclassical target and finds a quantum realization to machine precision. Distinguish an exact certified quantum construction from numerical agreement, and do not call the whole network problem solved by one topology/example. Priority: second tier; choose the exact residual instance after specialist examination of this manuscript.

## Verification and publication rules

- All 35 assigned entry texts were read, and the broader catalogue titles were checked for overlap. This audit is not a claim to have read every relevant paper or independently checked every existing reference.
- Primary literature, primary author/institutional material and author-maintained problem statements were used. Search snippets and secondary aggregators were discovery aids, not mathematical authorities. Recent arXiv abstract pages were checked for titles, authors, dates and claimed scope; selected full texts were used for sensitive claims such as Fu et al.'s provenance and the 2026 self-testing frontier.
- New July–August 2026 results are labeled as preprint claims unless a journal publication was confirmed. “Proved” in a manuscript abstract reports what its authors claim; it is not evidence of independent replication or proof checking by this audit.
- Absence of a general solution in the inspected primary sources is evidence for retaining a research question, not proof that it remains open. The strongest status checks combine recent explicit open-problem statements with scope checks of the newest nearby results. The two second-tier omission candidates are intentionally weaker recommendations.
- Do not infer a research cutoff from an arXiv identifier alone. For example, arXiv:2608.02647 records an initial submission on 31 July, and older identifiers can have materially important 2026 revisions. A future seminar announcement is not a past research event.
- AI-use disclosure belongs in a versioned provenance field, separate from theorem status. “No disclosure located in version X” does not imply “no AI was used”; conversely an explicit proof-development disclosure must not be rewritten as language editing only. No conclusion about correctness follows solely from the disclosed tool.
- Suggested implementation order: repair false assertions and citation metadata first; distinguish solved subcases from open residuals; consolidate E2, E15 and N9 into their parent topics and absorb M11 into C10; then add the highest-value concrete benchmarks. Preserve stable IDs/redirects and paper links when merging. No website data was changed by this audit.
