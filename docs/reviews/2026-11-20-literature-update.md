### Proof scope and residuals

**Gaussian optimality and capacities.** Gao–Adesso's Theorem 1 addresses the entire finite-mode bosonic target of E21, rather than only the older two-mode and bisymmetric cases. Proposition 2 and Appendix C compare against unrestricted states; Appendix D uses and strengthens interpolation results from the OpenAI entropy photon-number manuscript. Those external inputs were not separately re-audited here. For C10, the mean-energy-constrained classical capacity uses pure Gaussian signals with Gaussian displacement modulation and independence across channel uses; collective decoding is permitted. Theorem 4 treats sequential/parallel entanglement simulation cost, which is finite when the channel noise matrix is positive definite. These are separate tasks from quantum and private transmission capacity and from a strong converse. No full Lean claim is assigned to E21.

**Renormalization and holographic decoding.** R3's target asks for a symmetric positive-definite metric in a strict gradient representation. Hartman–Mathys–Stergiou's Section 8 gives a gradient-like relation with an antisymmetric curl term; a positive semidefinite spectral metric alone does not remove it. For H4, Waddell–Xu's lower bound Ω(2^(m/2)) and matching amplitude-amplification upper bound are statements about oracle queries. Oracle-independent computation is free. Their efficiently decodable post-selected stabilizer isometries explain why admissibility and low-order design properties alone cannot be substituted for Haar randomness. Neither paper closes its catalogue question.

**AdS stability.** Engelhardt–Nally's Section 3.3 proves a classical spherical AdS Penrose theorem under a weak fake-superpotential bound and specified falloffs. Section 6 works with eight real scalar fields, omitting the nine blowup moduli. Its global DGKT certificate combines an analytic exterior estimate and interval arithmetic in the interior; it is stronger than a search that found no decay. It excludes the scalar vacuum-decay channel in that effective theory, while omitted fields, corrections and all nonperturbative string mechanisms remain to be controlled. The scan of 66 KKLT vacua gives 10 Penrose-violating, 44 provisionally safe and 12 inconclusive cases. The safe label is restricted-search evidence. False-vacuum big-crunch evolution does not establish an observable naked singularity. None of these results resolves G10's quantum Penrose inequality.

**Memory and formalization.** Hayden's Part I establishes two boundary axes for a common three-layer code family: V = 0 at fixed sufficiently low positive temperature, and weak superpolynomially decaying V at T = 0. The thermal theorem controls KMS-norm drift and autocorrelation of decoded observables near equilibrium for bounded-rate Davies baths generated by local couplings. It does not provide passive preparation from a simple product state. The zero-temperature theorem controls the bulk gap, splitting and closed-system dressed memory. The full-spectrum form sandwich cannot alone transfer a rare-event Gibbs bound at fixed positive temperature, as Section 8.2 explains. The companion's interior result is therefore mentioned only as an announcement. Appendix A describes standalone existential Lean challenge conclusions, specialized regimes and approximate bounds; it explicitly does not identify every paper lemma with a formalized statement. No Lean build or certificate-source audit was performed here, and no new Lean badge is applied. The scale factor 5.4 × 10²⁴, estimated critical temperature no larger than about 10⁻⁵¹ and vanishing active-qubit density also distinguish this existence construction from a practical device.

**Numerics and restricted theorems.** B15 retains Open because a difference of two variational upper bounds is not a rigorous bound on the excitation gap. N2's new proof removes an input but establishes a weaker upper bound than the already recorded claim; it is not a new dimension-six resolution. N3's exact/interval certificate arguments exclude specified basis configurations, with availability limitations stated above. O2's Eq. 97 requires an integrated mixing estimate on the λ⁻² relaxation scale in addition to its bath and remainder hypotheses; without this, the displayed state-error estimate grows as O(λ² + λ⁴t). F8's locality statements concern a specified frame class and specified operations, rather than all physically realizable interventions.
